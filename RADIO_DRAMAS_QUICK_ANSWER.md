# 📻 Radio Dramas - Quick Answer

## Your Question:
> "Radio dramas, or radiosoaps, those audio plays from 1950's and such. Where do we file those properly?"

---

## THE ANSWER:

# **📻 Radio → 🎭 Old Time Radio**

---

## Why This Is Perfect:

### 1. It's Radio Content ✅
Radio dramas = Broadcast on radio = Goes in Radio section

### 2. It's Historical ✅
1930s-1960s golden age = "Old Time Radio" category

### 3. It's Episodic ✅
Organized like TV shows (series/episodes)

### 4. It's Distinct ✅
- **NOT** audiobooks (not books)
- **NOT** podcasts (not modern)
- **NOT** music (narrative audio)
- **IS** classic radio broadcasts!
