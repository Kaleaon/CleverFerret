HOW TO USE
===============================================================================

CLEVERFERRET ANDROID:
1. Open App → Fanfiction Downloader
2. Paste URL: https://metabods.com/stories/12345
3. Tap "Download as EPUB"
4. Done! ✅

CALIBRE DESKTOP:
1. Copy: cp calibre-config/fanficfare/personal.ini ~/.fanficfare/
2. Restart Calibre
3. Download from URL → Paste metabods.com URL
4. Done! ✅
